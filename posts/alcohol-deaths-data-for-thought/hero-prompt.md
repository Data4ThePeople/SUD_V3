# Hero image prompts: Alcohol: Less Alcoholism, More People Dying

Target: 1680x1080 landscape. Generate wide (16:10 or 3:2); the crop is done with
`~/.claude/d4tp-process/hero fit`. Keep the bottles and hand in the middle
two-thirds so a center crop does not cut them.

## Prompt 1 (home shelf, warm light)

Photorealistic editorial photograph, wide landscape format. A simple wooden
shelf against a plain dark wall holds two bottles side by side: on the left, a
brown glass beer bottle; on the right, a tall clear bottle of vodka. Both
bottles have plain labels with no text, logos or brand names. A single adult
hand enters from the right edge of the frame and reaches toward the vodka
bottle, fingers just about to close around its neck; the beer bottle is left
alone. Soft warm side light, shallow depth of field, the hand and vodka bottle
in sharp focus, the beer slightly softer. Quiet, serious mood. No people's
faces, no text, no watermark.

## Prompt 2 (store shelf)

Photorealistic photograph, wide landscape format, eye level with a liquor store
shelf. In the center of the shelf, a brown beer bottle stands next to a clear
spirits bottle; the rest of the shelf fades into soft blur. All labels are
blank, with no readable text, logos or brand names. An adult hand reaches in
from the right and is about to take the spirits bottle. Cool, even store
lighting with a slight overhead glow. Calm, documentary feel. No faces, no
text, no watermark.

## Prompt 3 (dark, to match the charts)

Photorealistic still life, wide landscape format, near-black background
(#181A1B) with a single soft key light from the left. A narrow dark shelf holds
a brown beer bottle on the left and a clear vodka bottle on the right, both with
blank labels and no text or logos. An adult hand reaches in from the right
toward the vodka bottle. Light catches the edge of the glass and the
knuckles; the beer bottle sits slightly in shadow. Minimal, serious,
high-contrast. Leave empty dark space across the top third. No faces, no
text, no watermark.

## Prompt 3b (dark, spirits made obvious) — use this one

Photorealistic still life, landscape format, 3:2 aspect ratio, near-black
background (#181A1B) with a single soft key light from the left. A narrow dark
wooden shelf holds two bottles a hand's width apart, both in the center of the
frame. On the left, a standard brown long-neck beer bottle with a metal crown
cap. On the right, a taller, heavier liquor bottle: broad square shoulders, a
short neck, a thick glass base and a cork stopper, two-thirds full of amber
whiskey that glows in the light. Both labels are blank, with no text, logos or
brand names. An adult hand reaches in from the right edge, fingers about to
close around the neck of the whiskey bottle; the whole hand and wrist stay
inside the frame. The beer bottle sits slightly in shadow. Minimal, serious,
high-contrast. Leave empty dark space across the top third. No faces, no
text, no watermark.
